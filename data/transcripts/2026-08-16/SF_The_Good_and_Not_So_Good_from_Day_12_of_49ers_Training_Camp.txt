The 49ers just finished day 12 of training camp. Here's who stood out. The good. Wide receiver Trenton Irwin. After De'Shawn Shivers stellar performance in his preseason debut a few days ago, it was reasonable to expect the Niners to reward him by giving him some opportunities with the starters today. They didn't. Instead, they gave those opportunities to Trenton Irwin, a 30-year-old wide receiver who caught two passes for 25 yards against the Titans on Thursday night. Apparently, the Niners really like what they see from him. Today, he caught a 30-yard pass and a 15-yard pass over the middle from Brock Purdy and later dropped a pass in the end zone. I'm guessing head coach Kyle Shanahan wanted to send Shivers a message that he hasn't arrived yet. What a silly message. He should be starting from now on and he shouldn't play any more in the preseason. He's too important. The Niners better not get him injured before the season starts. Wide receiver De'Shawn Shivers, he spent a few minutes at the beginning of practice working on catching passes that were thrown behind him because he dropped one of those on Thursday night. I guess the Niners weren't pleased about that mistake. Shanahan was decidedly lukewarm about Shivers in his postgame press conference. Today, Shanahan essentially demoted Shivers to the second string offense. He ran some routes with the starters, but Purdy never threw him the ball. Instead, Shivers caught passes from Mac Jones. I haven't seen a 49ers wide receiver unfairly punished like this since Brandon Aiyuk was in the doghouse 5 years ago. Free De'Shawn. Wide receiver Jacob Cowing, he returned from injury and beat Jack Jones to catch a 30-yard pass up the right sideline on a pass from Mac Jones. Cowing needs to stay healthy for the rest of camp and play like he did today in both preseason games. Otherwise, he probably won't make the team because he's not 30 like Irwin. Unfortunately for Cowing, he's merely 25, which is far younger than Shanahan would prefer. Wide receiver Deebo Samuel, another 30-year-old wide receiver the Niners just love. Today, he caught a 20-yard pass from Adrian Martinez over the middle. With the starters, Samuel didn't catch any passes, but he did carry the ball a couple times because all the top running backs are injured. Give Samuel credit. He's He's the best shape possible and he shows up every day as opposed to lots of other veterans on the team. He's one of the Iron Mans of camp so far, which is incredible. Unfortunately for him, he's 30, which means he's not as fast as he once was. Good thing Shanahan loves him. Tight end Luke Farrell, he caught three passes from Purdy during a move the ball drill. Purdy seems to trust him, which is surprising considering Farrell was awful last year. He's having a solid camp. Cornerback Renardo Green, he nearly intercepted a deep pass Brock Purdy intended for Mike Evans. Evans had a step on Green, but the trajectory of Purdy's pass was low and caused Evan to slow down, which allowed Green to recover and knock the pass away. Ideally, it would have been nice to see Green intercept that pass because it hit both of his hands. Unfortunately, he doesn't have good hands, but he still is a good cornerback. Cornerback Jack Jones, he injured his hand early on Thursday night against the Titans, but practiced today and recovered a fumble. He takes the ball away from the offense seemingly every other practice, but his coverage isn't exactly sticky. He's an enigma. Linebacker Jaden Dogger, he started at strong side linebacker, then moved to weak side linebacker next to Fred Warner in the nickel defense because Dre Greenlaw had the day off. The 9ers clearly love Dogger, and I'm guessing he'll start at SAM in week one. If Greenlaw ever misses significant time, I expect Dogger will replace him at will and play 100% of the snaps. His stock is soaring. Offensive tackle Trent Williams, he shows up every day and practices even though he's a 38-year-old future Hall of Famer who doesn't really need the work. I've never seen him lead like this before. I'm guessing he's positively reinforcing the 9ers for extending his contract this off season, which is smart. The not so good. Brock Purdy's connection with Mike Evans, it's a work in progress. To be fair, Evans missed at least a week with a quad strain, and today was his first full day back. And he made a nice sliding catch over the middle on a pass from Purdy. But later, Purdy misjudged Evan's speed, underthrew him, and nearly got picked off. Finally, Purdy fired a deep throw toward the sideline before Evans made his break, and the pass landed out of bounds. Purdy loves throwing anticipatory passes to receivers before they've turned their head, but he can't do that just yet with Evans because they haven't played together much. Right now Purdy has a much stronger connection with Trenton Irwin who shouldn't make the team. Quarterback Mac Jones, I don't think I've said anything nice about him yet in camp. I wanted to praise him today, but then he threw an interception right to Jalen Shermond, another player who won't make the team. It'll be nice to see Jones in the preseason game against the Chargers on Thursday considering he didn't play against the Titans and has yet to impress in camp. Chris Wrock has played much better than Jones so far. Left guard Connor Colbie. He was promoted to starting left guard after playing backup right guard for the past week. That's the good news. The not so good news is that Colbie was awful and routinely gave up penetration on runs and passes to Osa Odighizuwa. The Niners simply don't have a starting caliber left guard. Robert Jones certainly doesn't qualify. He's worse than Colbie. Perhaps rookie Carver Willis can take his job eventually, but he has a long way to go.
