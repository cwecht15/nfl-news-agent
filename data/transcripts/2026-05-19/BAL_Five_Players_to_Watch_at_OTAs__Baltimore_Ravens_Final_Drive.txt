Happy Monday, Ravens flock. The Ravens started organized team activities, OTAAS, today as the next phase of the offseason program begins. This phase contains voluntary onfield practices, which the team can hold 10 of before mandatory camp in June. It's the first time the rookies will be participating with the vets and organized team activities and all eyes will be on first round pick Manga Awan as he works to build rapport with the Ravens starting offensive line and nail down timing with the Ravens offense throughout the offseason program. It will also be important for rookie receivers Jacobe Lane and Elijah Serat to get a feel for operating in a Lamar Jackson run offense and Jackson will learn his new weapons tendencies and strengths as the summer progresses. Perhaps not as glamorous of a pick, but punter Ryan Eckley will be expected to come in and be effective right away, replacing Pro Bowler Jordan Stout, who departed in free agency. And in tonight's episode of Wired, senior special teams coach Randy Brown refers to Ekley as a young Sam Cook, the Ravens highly respected punter of 16 years. So, big shoes to fill. And coming into his sophomore season, I'm excited to see what safety Malachi Starks brings into year two. Starks played in all 17 games as a rookie and is a player I expect to see take a big jump with a full season's experience under his belt. And finally, who isn't excited to see Trey Hendrickson on the field in a Ravens uniform? We got to see him during veteran mini camp earlier this off season, but it's still feels surreal. Hendrickson has been a fixture in the building throughout the offseason program, and after ending his last season with Cincinnati Uninjured Reserve, it's clear Hendrickson is itching to get back to real action. Something I'm sure head coach and defensive play caller Jesse Mentor is also looking forward to.
