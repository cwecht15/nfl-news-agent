When NFL players win the Super Bowl, they go to Disney World. When soccer players win the World Cup, apparently they go to Owings Mills, Maryland. That was the scene at Ravens training camp today as Spain's hero Feron Torres made a special appearance at practice. And for one day, the most famous footballer in Maryland wasn't wearing a purple jersey. Torres is in the United States on a press tour and he made a pretty memorable stop at the Ravens training camp today speaking to the team during the final huddle taking photos and spending time with players and coaches and he even left with some new Ravens gear. Torres exchanged cleats with Zay Flowers who is fresh off signing his new contract extension with Baltimore and he also swapped jerseys with Ronnie Stanley. Head coach Jesse Mentor pointed to Torres's World Cup performance as a perfect example of what it means to be ready when your number is called. &gt;&gt; We had a uh very special guest today, Fon Torres of Spain, game-winning goal in the World Cup. And uh it just kind of like really cool message. This guy came off the bench in the finals of the World Cup um in extra time, scored the game-winning goal. talk about just being ready when your number's called, being at your best when your best is needed. Uh, I mean, that's like the ultimate. So, it was uh such an honor and a pleasure to have him. &gt;&gt; That's a message that certainly fits a football team getting ready for a new season. You may not know exactly when your opportunity is coming. You may not know exactly what your role will look like, but when your number is called, you better be ready. And who better to deliver that message than a World Cup champion who knows exactly what that moment feels like. &gt;&gt; I think that says a lot uh for him, you know, being ready when your number's called. Um I think that can say a lot for, you know, a lot of people in this world, you know, just, you know, staying prepared, you know, cuz you just never know when your number's called. So, um even for myself, I never know when my number is going to be called. So, I got to stay ready at all times. For one day, Fon Torres traded the pitch for the practice field, got a taste of Ravens football, and left with a couple of pieces of purple to remember the day by.
