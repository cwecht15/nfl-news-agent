With just over a month to go until the NFL Draft, the pre-draft process is hitting another key phase. Now that the combine has come and gone, attention across the league now shifts to college pro days, a crucial part of the evaluation process where teams get another opportunity to see prospects up close. And that brings us to Penn State's pro day yesterday. Around 20 prospects took part in drills and measurements, but one of the most notable sights was new Ravens offensive line coach Dwayne Ledford right in the middle of the action. Ledford was heavily involved in position drills for the offensive lineman, getting an up-close look at a group that includes one of the most talked about potential fits for Baltimore, guard Vederian Lowe. Lowe is a name that's been consistently linked to the Ravens in mock drafts, and it's easy to see why. &gt;&gt; [music] &gt;&gt; At 6'4 and over 320 lb, he brings size, power, and the kind of physicality that fits perfectly with Baltimore's identity in the run game.
