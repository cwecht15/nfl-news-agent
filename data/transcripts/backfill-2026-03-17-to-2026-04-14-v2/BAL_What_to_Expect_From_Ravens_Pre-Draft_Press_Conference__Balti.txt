The NFL Draft is just over a week away and Wednesday we will get a sneak peek at how the Ravens brass view this draft class. General Manager Eric DeCosta and Jesse Minter will address the media to preview their strategy. Starting with pick number 14, DeCosta will have 11 picks at his disposal barring any trades. Now often called the liars luncheon by media as DeCosta plays his cards close to his vest [music] a week out from the draft lest another team get wind of who the Ravens favor. It will be DeCosta's first draft building a roster for new head coach Jesse Minter. But DeCosta said at the combine he doesn't anticipate that changing who the Ravens draft as they stick to their best player available mantra.
