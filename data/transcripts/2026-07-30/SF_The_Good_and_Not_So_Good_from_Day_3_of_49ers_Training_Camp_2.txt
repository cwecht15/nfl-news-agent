The 49ers just finished day three of training camp. Here's who stood out. Keep in mind Ricky Pearsall missed practice with knee swelling, an injury that is related to his PCL which he hurt last year. Apparently it still hasn't recovered and the 9ers are concerned. The good. Wide receiver De'Marcus Robinson, he took Pearsall's place as a starting Z receiver and caught three passes from Brock Purdy including a leaping grab over the middle while running a dig route. Do not be surprised if Robinson finished the season as one of the 9ers most productive wide receivers even though he's almost 32. That's because he's durable and he has chemistry with the starting quarterback. Plus he's entering his second season in Kyle Shanahan's offensive system which gives him an advantage over the rookie. Wide receiver D'Joun Sturdivant, he caught five passes from four different quarterbacks including a leaping grab over the middle on a high pass from Adrian Martinez. Sturdivant isn't Brandon Aiyuk or Ricky Pearsall when it comes to running routes, but the 9ers are finding ways to scheme him open and get him the ball on screens, slants, and digs. Expect Expect him to have a highly productive rookie season even if he's not the number one receiver on the team. Wide receiver Jordan Watkins, he caught four passes running four different routes, a dig, an out, a slant, and a fade. Before practice offensive coordinator Clay Kubiak praised Watkins for improving since last season when he was a rookie. Right now he looks like a quality backup receiver with potential to be better and the primary punt returner. If he can stay healthy he'll get opportunities to catch passes. Linebacker Dre Greenlaw, he seems 100% healthy for the first time in almost three years. He stripped the ball from Christian McCaffrey, recovered it, and ran it into the end zone on the first play of practice, then recorded a tackle for loss later on. One healthy Greenlaw is the heart and soul of the 9ers defense. He's been a more impactful addition so far than Mike Evans. More on him in a minute. Cornerback Jack Jones, he intercepted his second pass in three days of camp. Today he intercepted a pass that was intended for Jacob Cowing and thrown by Mac Jones. More on him in a minute.
