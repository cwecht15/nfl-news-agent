The 49ers just wrapped up day 11 of training camp, which was a joint practice against the Tennessee Titans. Here's who stood out. Keep in mind that after practice, head coach Kyle Shanahan said Nick Bosa is dealing with knee tendinitis that is related to his ACL recovery. More on Bosa in a minute. The good wide receiver Dejon Stribbling. He outplayed Titans rookie wide receiver Carnell Tate, who was the fourth pick in the draft. Tate didn't catch many passes, and when he did make a catch, he often had to lay out as opposed to dribbling, who caught a team high seven passes during 11on1 team drills and caught them in stride. He was Brock Pery's go-to guy, particularly outside the red zone, where dribbling speed stretches the field and stresses defenses. Quarterbacks give him such a big cushion, which opens up underneath routes for him. He's a terrific compliment for Demarcus Robinson, who's more of a possession receiver and red zone specialist. Stribbling isn't currently a huge threat in the red zone, but he could develop that part of his game in time. Expect him to get a ton of playing time and targets this season as long as he's healthy. Wide receiver Demarcus Robinson. He's not particularly fast, but he's big and tough and dependable, similar to Jawan Jennings. Robinson is also fearless over the middle. He has a big catch radius, and he's at his best in the end zone where he made two catches today. Theoretically, Mike Evans can do all these things even better, but he's been injured for most of camp while Robinson hasn't missed a day. Don't be surprised if Robinson outproduces Evans this season simply because Evans is older and breaking down. Tight end Jake Tongis. Safety Gier Brown. He undercut and intercepted a pass over the middle thrown by Cam Ward. Quarterback Renardo Green. He's not going to intercept passes, and he will get beaten by practice wide players occasionally during one-on-one drills, but he's also the cornerback on the 49ers who's best suited to match up with number one wide receivers because of his size and speed. And today he used those traits to shut down rookie first round pick Carnell Tate. We've also seen Green shut down Pro Bowlers such as Devonte Adams as recently as last year.
