The 49ers just lost 19 to 13 to the Tennessee Titans in preseason game number one. Here are their grades. Quarterbacks C++. Curtis Ror outplayed Cam Ward until he injured his rib running between the tackles trying to score a touchdown in a preseason game. More on that decision in a minute. Ror finished the night completing 12 of 14 pass attempts for 101 yards and posted a respectable pass rating of 96.7. He saw the field well, got rid of the ball quickly, avoided a sack, scrambled for a first down, and made a beautiful deep throw to Dejon, dribbling up the right sideline. Ror looks like he has potential to develop into a quality backup or maybe even a starter. Too bad the Niners got him injured. His replacement, Adrien Martinez, was awful. Running backs D. None of these guys will make the team, nor should they. They're all mere camp bodies. The best one tonight was Sincere McCormack, who was quick and ran hard, but averaged a mere 3.8 8 yards per carry. In fairness to him and the other running backs, who aren't really worth naming, they kept getting hit in the back field, which means they never really had a chance. Wide receivers, Bminus, Dejon's dribbling was by far the Niners's best receiver, but that's not saying much considering all their veterans sat out. That made dribbling the focal point of the offense, and he responded well, catching seven of eight targets for 63 yards. He doesn't create a ton of separation, which means he might not be the best option to move the chains on short passes, but he has outstanding hands. He catches the ball far away from his frame and he's a true deep threat on a team that needs one. It'll be interesting to see if he opens up Brock Pie's downfield passing game. The second best wide receiver in the game for the Niners was Jordan Watkins who caught six passes for 58 yards. Tight ends F. It made almost no impact. It's really too bad the Niners haven't drafted a tight end since Cam Lu. Offensive lineman D. The Niners have a real problem at left guard considering they don't have a starter at that position. The favorite to start is Robert Jones, who played tonight and was awful both in the run game and the pass game, and he's their best option. The Niners need rookie Carver Willis to grow up quickly and take his job. He played most of the second half and performed better than Jones, which isn't saying much. Defensive lineman D. Rookie Grayson Halton was the star of this group. He stood up double teams in the run game and recorded a tackle for loss and a sack that was called back due to a penalty away from the play. The rest of the defensive line was horrible, though. Alfred Collins got pushed around and Romelo height made no impact as a run defender or a pass rusher. In fact, veteran camp body Abana Okaranquo was much more impressive than height, which is troubling. Once again, the Niners will be extremely reliant on Nick Bosa to be durable and dominant, two things he might not be able to provide anymore. Linebackers C plus Tatum Bthun was solid as usual. He's an excellent backup middle linebacker. Nick Martin played most of the game, which probably isn't a good sign for his chances to start at outside linebacker. Maybe the Niners wanted to showcase him to the Titans so they'll trade for him considering Robert Salah drafted him last year. Martin's biggest play was a 15-y penalty against him. He still doesn't seem to know what he's doing as opposed to rookie Jaden Duggar who didn't play like a rookie. Don't be surprised if he becomes a Niners third linebacker by the end of the season. Defensive backs B. Jack Jones left the game early with a hand injury. Rookie Ephesians Pric replaced him and promptly broke up a potential touchdown pass intended for rookie firstround pick Carnell Tate in the front right corner of the end zone. Pric engulfed him, made him look tiny. He has serious potential. None of the other defensive backs stood out with the exception of Ashton Davis who nearly intercepted Cam Ward. Special teams C. The Niners have an outstanding kicker tonight. Eddie Panero made a 52-y field goal. Easy, like breathing. Unfortunately for the Niners, they don't have a punt returner. Coaches D. I usually don't grade the coaches during the preseason because they don't game plan. They're coaching to evaluate certain players, not to win. And yet, for some strange reason, Kyle Shanahan and Klay Kubak decided to call a quarterback draw for Curtis Ror from the four-yd line. It's always risky to run a quarterback between the tackles near the goal line. And on this play, Ror injured his ribs. It's unclear how much time he'll miss. Fortunately, the Niners also have M. Jones to back up Rock Purdy. But Ror seems extremely promising for a young quarterback. He's big, he's accurate, and he's mobile. Now the Niners just have to keep him healthy. Easier said than done for them. [music]
