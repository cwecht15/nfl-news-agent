The 49ers just finished their 17th and final day of training camp. Here's who stood out. The good, wide receiver De'Jean Shriblin. He's playing through a right shoulder injury and he's still the best wide receiver on the team and it's not close. Today he lost his one-on-one match up against Jack Jones who broke up the pass when Shriblin was running deep. After that rep, Kyle Shanahan called Shriblin over to him and gave him some coaching points. Whatever Shanahan said worked because Shriblin went on to dominate practice with one healthy shoulder. He caught four passes for roughly 80 yards during team drills. After every catch, he awkwardly flipped the ball back to a coach with his left hand because his right shoulder hurts. The 9ers need him to stay healthy all season and catch at least 70 passes. If you can do that, they'll win lots of games. Running back Christian McCaffrey, he practiced for the second day in a row and looked perfectly healthy. He caught three passes and carried the ball many, many times. I believe McCaffrey when he says he wasn't injured and the 9ers simply agreed to let him skip two weeks of camp. I think he wants a new deal and he's salty that the 9ers didn't give him one. Who knows, maybe they want to extend McCaffrey but haven't been able to get Jet York on the phone. More on York in a minute. Tight end Luke Farrell, he caught a touchdown pass from Brock Purdy in the red zone. Farrell is having a good camp which means the 9ers have three good veteran tight ends, him, George Kittle, and Jake Tonges. Those three should play alongside McCaffrey and Shriblin as much as possible. Forget Deebo Samuel and Kyle Juszczyk and use Mike Evans sparingly. If the 9ers overuse Evans, he won't make it through the season. Safety Marqui Seagal, he intercepted a pass that Purdy threw over the middle to Demarcus Robinson. The pass was high, Robinson jumped and tipped the ball into the air, Seagal dove and came down with it. Purdy is having a good camp but whenever he misses, he tends to miss high which is why he liked Juwan Jennings so much. Jennings would have caught this pass. Robinson isn't as tall so he tipped it to the defense. Safety Jaquiski Brown, he broke up a pass that was intended for Deebo Samuel during one-on-one drills. I could put Samuel in the not-so-good section but I want to be positive so I'll focus on Brown shutting down a wide receiver. It's always impressive when a safety does that. Defensive end Sam Okuwanu. He beat starting right tackle Colton McKivitz around the edge to sack Purdy during 11-on-11 team drills. Okuwanu has taken practically all the first team reps at defensive end at camp. That's because Nick Bosa hasn't participated at all and Romello Height has been in and out. Today he missed practice with an ankle injury he suffered against the Chargers. The not-so-good. Owner Jed York, he did not show up to practice. Apparently he lives in Ohio now and hasn't been around the facility in a while, which is odd. Didn't his parents turn the team over to him because they didn't want to live in California and run the day-to-day operations? Isn't that what York is supposed to be doing? Can he really run the team from Ohio while he's dealing with humiliating legal issues? Because for the past month team CEO Al Guido has been at practically every practice. If you didn't know better, you'd think Guido was the Niners owner. Maybe he will be soon. Quarterback Brock Purdy, he got intercepted trying to force a pass over the middle. Defensive coordinator Raheem Morris affectionately calls Purdy the layer king because of his ability to float balls over the middle with touch. This off-season those passes are often sailing a bit too high. Surprisingly Purdy's best throws have gone outside the numbers. He really misses George Kittle. Fortunately for Purdy, Kittle will be back soon. Cornerback Jack Jones, he gave up a 30-yard catch to De'Shawn Shribling then pushed him to the ground after the play. Keep in mind Shribling has a shoulder injury and was wearing a black non-contact jersey, which means Jones wasn't supposed to touch him, let alone push him to the ground. Shribling stayed on the ground for a few seconds and got up slowly. The team needs to fine Jones for that mental mistake. Nickelback Qwuantrezz Knight Stout, he missed practice with a groin injury. Deommodore Lenoir took his place at nickelback. No disrespect to Stout, but Lenoir is better at that position. Wide receiver Mike Evans, he missed yet another practice this time with groin tightness, which means Evans missed more than half of training camp this year. He never went more than a few practices in a row without getting injured. I expect this season will play out similarly. Wide receiver Jordan Watkins, he lost both of his reps during one-on-one drills. First to Samuel then to Jack Jones. Both times Watkins ran a deep comeback route and created no separation. They needed the same thing during 11-on-11 team drills and the pass got broken up. Watkins is a good athlete, but he can't get himself open or make contested catches, which means he's a system guy and a backup. Wide receiver Jacob Cowing, he missed practice with a groin injury. Like Evans, Cowing can't go more than a few days without tweaking something. Unlike Evans, Cowing isn't a future Hall of Famer. He still should make the team.
