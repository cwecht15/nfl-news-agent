Myles Garrett is out of the AFC North. A blockbuster trade rocked the NFL today as the Browns traded Garrett to the Los Angeles Rams. Here's my reaction to the move and how it impacts the Ravens. Garrett's not only the reigning Defensive Player of the Year, but he also set the single-season sack record last season. He has the most career sacks of any active player in the NFL. Even at 30 years old, Garrett is still a freak of nature that is in the prime of his career. And he's been a thorn in the Ravens' side. In two games last season against Baltimore, he had five and a half sacks and 12 pressures. In 16 career games against the Ravens, he had 14 sacks. Getting Garrett out of the AFC North means no longer facing him twice a season. Garrett and Ravens left tackle Ronnie Stanley had many good battles over the years, but you have to think Stanley is smiling somewhere. Now, on the flip side, the Browns got a haul for trading Garrett. According to ESPN's Adam Schefter, the Browns are getting young star pass rusher Jared Verse, a 2027 first-round pick, a 2028 second-round pick, and 2029 third-round pick. Now, Verse is a good player in his own regard. He's been a Pro Bowler each of his two years in the league, and he was the AP Defensive Rookie of the Year in 2024. He had seven and a half sacks last year, and only Garrett and Micah Parsons have had more quarterback pressures since Verse entered the league. In the Ravens game against the Rams last year, Verse had a sack, two tackles for loss, and four quarterback hits. He's a very strong player in his own right and 25 years old, so a lot of years against him for the Ravens most likely. Now, the Browns are stocking up with more draft picks as they build for the future. That 2027 first-round pick will most likely be a late one, however. The Rams have now vaulted ahead of the Ravens as Vegas' favorite to win next year's Super Bowl. The Ravens landed a premier pass rusher in Trey Hendrickson this offseason. The Browns shipped one out in exchange for an up-and-coming talent and draft picks. The AFC North continues to make big moves and we just kicked off June. What's coming next?
