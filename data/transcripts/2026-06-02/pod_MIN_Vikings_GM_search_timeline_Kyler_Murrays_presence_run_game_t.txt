Alec Lewis, the Vikings beat writer at The Athletic and host of The Alec Lewis Show, answers listener questions as he does at the beginning of every week. Lewis discusses the timeline for the Vikings’ GM search, Kyler Murray’s presence in Minnesota, the tweaks the Vikings are making to the run game and much more. Lewis gives his thoughts on the team hosting the 2028 NFL Draft and even hits the subject of Christian Darrisaw.
