The Ravens have wrapped up their first week of OTAs and there have been some early standouts. First round pick Binka Awani has been as advertised. Jessie Minter called him a beast and said the rookie has picked up everything faster than he'd expect at any position. Veteran guard John Simpson called the rookie a sponge and at another spot on the offensive line, center Danny Pinter took reps with the first team and coach Minter said he did a great job. Pinter will continue to rotate through the off-season program with Joe Von Gwenn and Corey Bullock as that competition takes shape during training camp. From an energy standpoint, you can't miss rookie receiver Jacoby Lane on the practice field. You might have seen his backflip, but he was also getting noticed for his play making on the field with offensive coordinator Decklin Doyle taking note. And with some of the veteran corners out, the younger players in the secondary got more reps and Robert Longerbeam who was sidelined for his rookie year with a knee injury took full advantage having an interception and a pass break up. Longerbeam will work to carve out a role in the talented group over the summer. And earlier this week I named safety Malachi Starcs as one of my players to watch during OTAs and the sophomore hasn't disappointed. During this OTA portion, it's a teammate first mentality from a physicality standpoint, but Starcs seems to be in the right spot to make a play often. It was a week of high energy as Jessie Minter establishes his culture as a head coach and for more OTA standouts visit our website. Have a great Memorial Day weekend everyone. We'll be back with you next week.
