Let's get into it. The players are broken up into nine groups by position and operate off a staggered schedule. That means everyone has the same itinerary over a 5-day span, but they start on different days. The Dline, linebackers, and specialists kick off the week on Monday, and the O line closes out the combine on Sunday. The first two days of a player's week are filled with orientation and medical exams, which can be the most crucial part of the week for players coming off a major injury or players with an extensive injury history.
