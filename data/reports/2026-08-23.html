**Today’s NFL injury briefing**

**Biggest developments**
- **Cardinals:** P **Blake Gillikin** hurt his groin in warm-ups, was shut down for the day, and will get an MRI Sunday. - **Why it matters:** New missed-time concern for Kansas City.

**What to watch next**
- Gillikin’s MRI
- Final word on Taylor-Demerson and Sewell
- Whether Stribling, Lenoir, and the 49ers’ other day-to-day players can get back on the field
