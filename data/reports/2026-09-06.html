### Key return to watch: Daniel Jones, Colts
- **Status:** Returned from the **Achilles tear he suffered in Week 14 last season**.
- **Camp update:** He **didn’t miss a single camp practice in August** and handled **roughly 100% of the first-team reps**.
- **Why it matters:** That’s the clearest sign yet that Jones is back to being the Colts’ full-time quarterback without major restrictions. It also matters for an offense that was explosive early last season — **32.1 points per game in the first 10 games** before Jones got hurt.
- **What to watch next:** Whether the Colts’ offense can sustain that level of production and whether Jones stays fully healthy once games begin.
